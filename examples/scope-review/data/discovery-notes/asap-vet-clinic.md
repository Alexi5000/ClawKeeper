# Discovery call: Juniper Hollow Animal Clinic (fictional)

Client: Juniper Hollow Animal Clinic
Industry: veterinary clinic
Contact: Priya Okonkwo-Lane, practice manager (fictional)
Decision maker: Dr. Tomas Reyburn, owner (fictional)
Budget: $15,000
Delivery date: ASAP
Users: front desk team (5) and two on-call vets
Integrations: practice management system, shared Google calendar
Success criteria: no missed after-hours calls; booked appointments show up in the morning schedule

## What we heard
- Two sites. After 6pm the phones go to a voicemail box nobody checks until morning.
- They lose new-client bookings to the clinic down the road because nobody answers.
- Owner wants a voice receptionist that answers after-hours calls, books routine appointments and flags emergencies.
- Emergencies should go to whoever is on-call that night; the rota lives in a spreadsheet.
- "We need this ASAP" came up three times. No date was given when asked.
